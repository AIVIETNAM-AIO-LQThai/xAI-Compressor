# AeroXAI Cross-Dataset Research Synthesis

Status: **research branch only**

Branch: `research/multi-dataset-validation`

This document synthesizes the validated research checkpoints performed after
the frozen `esic-2026-v3` release. It does not modify the frozen product
release or promote any exploratory detector into production.

## Research question

The research began from a concrete concern: MetroPT explanations generated by
the frozen Robust-PCA detector were often dominated by only one or two
operator-level signal groups.

The initial question was:

> Is the apparent explanation sparsity an artifact of MetroPT-3, an intrinsic
> property of PCA reconstruction, or a broader interaction among data,
> representation, scaling, sensor availability, and time?

The evidence now supports the broader interaction interpretation.

## Evidence hierarchy

The studies below must remain separated by provenance.

### REAL telemetry / recordings

- MetroPT-3 historical APU process telemetry.
- MetroPT2 historical APU process telemetry.
- IITK Air Compressor acoustic recordings with seeded health-state faults.

### SIMULATED physics/control

- AeroXAI inverse-state physics.
- bounded physical uncertainty.
- robust compressor dispatch.
- proof-carrying 60-second advisory action.

The REAL detector studies do not calibrate the synthetic compressor twin to
MetroPT or IITK. The SIMULATED control chain therefore remains a separate
validation path.

---

## 1. Frozen MetroPT-3 baseline

The frozen Robust-PCA detector uses:

```text
RobustScaler
PCA reconstruction error
5-minute causal feature bins
calibration-only thresholding
causal EWMA
3-of-4 persistence
```

The frozen detector detected all four documented high-stress air-leak
incidents within the predefined timely window:

```text
4 / 4 timely
2 / 4 pre-onset
2 / 4 shortly after onset
false alert rate ≈ 0.886 / day
PR-AUC ≈ 0.249
```

Approved wording remains:

> PCA detected all four documented incidents within the predefined timely
> window; two produced pre-onset warnings and two were detected shortly after
> onset.

Do not claim all four incidents were predicted in advance.

### Frozen explanation concentration

At the four incident explanation timestamps, the frozen detector evidence was
highly concentrated:

```text
mean top-3 contribution share ≈ 0.99996
mean effective group count N_eff ≈ 1.72
range N_eff ≈ 1.26 to 2.10
```

This describes detector evidence, not physical fault complexity.

## 2. MetroPT2 cross-period / cross-fault replication

MetroPT2 was introduced as a related but richer Porto Metro benchmark.

The frozen preprocessing protocol used three sensor variants:

```text
full
flowmeter_excluded
strict_flow_blind
```

and evaluated an air-leak event plus an oil-leak event.

### Main result

Robust-PCA explanation concentration persisted across:

- a different MetroPT recording period,
- two fault labels,
- inclusion/exclusion of Flowmeter,
- stricter flow-blind variants.

This ruled out the simple explanation that MetroPT-3 alone caused the
concentrated detector evidence.

Approved conclusion:

> Multi-dataset evidence did not support the hypothesis that MetroPT-3 alone
> caused the highly concentrated explanations. Robust-PCA reconstruction
> remained strongly concentrated across MetroPT2 air-leak and oil-leak events
> and across flow-sensor ablations.

Flowmeter materially improved ranking performance in the full MetroPT2
variant, but the first timely Robust-PCA explanation remained pressure
dominated.

## 3. Representation and scaling ablation

A frozen 2×2 representation/scaling study compared:

```text
RobustScaler   + PCA reconstruction
StandardScaler + PCA reconstruction
RobustScaler   + direct feature energy
StandardScaler + direct feature energy
```

across all three MetroPT2 sensor variants.

Two timestamp views were used:

1. each representation's own first timely alert;
2. the frozen Robust-PCA timely-alert timestamp.

The matched-timestamp comparison was essential because explanation
dimensionality changed sharply with time.

### Main result

The evidence did **not** support:

> PCA projection alone causes explanation collapse.

Instead, concentration depended on:

```text
data geometry
scaling
representation
sensor availability
explanation timestamp
```

Important examples included:

- Standard-PCA often produced less concentrated evidence than Robust-PCA at
  matched timestamps.
- Standard feature-energy exposed a highly distributed oil-leak state in the
  flow-blind setting at its own early alert.
- The same representation could appear highly distributed at one timestamp
  and highly concentrated at another.
- Full-feature Standard methods frequently became dominated by Flowmeter,
  demonstrating a potential sensor-shortcut effect.

Approved conclusion:

> Explanation concentration was not invariant to the analysis pipeline.
> Robust scaling and PCA reconstruction both increased concentration in
> several matched-time comparisons, while sensor availability and explanation
> timestamp materially changed the apparent dimensionality of anomaly
> evidence.

## 4. Temporal explanation dynamics

The next experiment traced explanation concentration over four fixed
incident-relative windows:

```text
-24h to -6h   early precursor
 -6h to  0h   near precursor
  0h to +2h   early event
 +2h to +6h   established event
```

Across flow-blind Standard-PCA and Standard feature-energy, both MetroPT2
faults often showed broad evidence before onset and much more concentrated
evidence during the persistent event.

However, high N_eff also occurred during non-alerting periods.

### Main result

Therefore:

> High-dimensional evidence alone is not a precursor.

The relevant object is the joint behavior of:

```text
anomaly magnitude
+
explanation spread
```

rather than explanation spread alone.

## 5. Calibration-normalized evidence states

A calibration-relative state analysis then classified each bin using two axes.

Magnitude:

```text
subthreshold
threshold_crossing
alerting
```

Spread:

```text
concentrated   <= calibration N_eff percentile 0.10
typical
diffuse        >= calibration N_eff percentile 0.90
```

### Hypothesis tested

A candidate temporal story was:

```text
subthreshold|diffuse
→ threshold_crossing|diffuse
→ alerting|concentrated
```

### Result

The universal transition hypothesis failed.

Across the tested variant / representation / incident combinations, the
active alert did not exhibit a consistent `alerting|diffuse →
alerting|concentrated` sequence.

Some cases showed `alerting|typical → alerting|concentrated`, but this was not
universal.

### Scientific value of the negative result

This falsification prevented overfitting an HMM, Markov model, or learned
state-transition narrative to only two repeatedly inspected MetroPT2
incidents.

Approved conclusion:

> Calibration-normalized evidence states are useful descriptors, but MetroPT2
> does not demonstrate one universal temporal state sequence. Different
> representations enter the alerting regime as either typical or concentrated
> evidence; diffuse evidence occurs mainly while anomaly magnitude is still
> subthreshold.

## 6. Independent IITK compressor/acoustic validation

The IITK Air Compressor Health State Dataset provided the first independent
cross-asset and cross-modality stress test.

Dataset structure:

```text
8 health states
225 recordings per state
1,800 recordings total
50,000 scalar acoustic values per recording
```

Conditions:

```text
Healthy
Bearing
Flywheel
LIV
LOV
NRV
Piston
Riderbelt
```

The audit verified all 1,800 recordings were finite and had identical length.

### Frozen split

Before detector results were viewed:

```text
Healthy Reading001-120   train
Healthy Reading121-180   calibration
Healthy Reading181-225   test
All 1,575 fault recordings   test only
```

Each recording was converted into 31 deterministic acoustic features grouped
into five explanation families:

```text
amplitude
distribution_shape
temporal_structure
spectral_shape
spectral_band_energy
```

Absolute frequency in Hz was intentionally not used because the sample rate
of the exact preprocessed files was not treated as verified.

### Detection result

Frozen operating-point results:

| Representation | ROC-AUC | Healthy FPR | Fault recall | Balanced accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Robust-PCA | 1.0000 | 0.0% | 99.746% | 99.873% |
| Standard-PCA | 1.0000 | 0.0% | 99.937% | 99.968% |
| Standard feature-energy | 0.99898 | 0.0% | 94.857% | 97.429% |
| Robust feature-energy | 0.97027 | 2.22% | 38.095% | 67.937% |

### Explanation result

Unlike MetroPT, IITK PCA explanations remained substantially distributed
across the five acoustic feature families.

Therefore the external result directly falsified:

> PCA reconstruction intrinsically produces concentrated explanations.

The supported interpretation is:

> Explanation concentration is a property of the interaction among dataset
> geometry, features, scaling, representation, fault/state, and timestamp,
> rather than an intrinsic property of PCA reconstruction.

## 7. IITK statistical uncertainty and calibration stability

A preregistered follow-up quantified finite-sample uncertainty without changing
the split, feature representation, or q=0.95 operating rule.

Analyses:

```text
Wilson 95% reference intervals
leave-one-calibration-recording-out thresholds
5,000 IID calibration bootstraps
5,000 circular block-5 calibration bootstraps
5,000 circular block-10 calibration bootstraps
1,000 stratified ROC-AUC bootstraps
```

### Robust-PCA

Robust-PCA remained operationally stable under threshold resampling.

Typical bootstrap behavior:

```text
fault recall ≈ 98.7% to 100%
healthy FPR median = 0%
ROC-AUC bootstrap ≈ complete observed separation
```

This does not prove population AUC is exactly 1.

### Standard-PCA

Standard-PCA showed similarly strong operating stability.

### Standard feature-energy

Standard feature-energy had excellent ranking but noticeably larger threshold
sensitivity.

Its frozen operating recall remained high, but calibration resampling produced
a substantially wider recall distribution than PCA.

### Robust feature-energy

Robust feature-energy had two distinct weaknesses:

```text
weaker ranking
+
unstable / conservative calibration threshold
```

Frozen:

```text
ROC-AUC ≈ 0.9703
fault recall ≈ 38.10%
healthy FPR ≈ 2.22%
threshold ≈ 15.57
```

Its calibration upper tail contained a strong outlier:

```text
Reading164 ≈ 49.59
Reading132 ≈ 16.96
Reading125 ≈ 15.57  <- frozen q=.95 threshold
```

IID calibration bootstrap thresholds varied widely and produced a broad
operating-recall distribution.

This means the poor operating recall cannot be attributed solely to a bad
fixed threshold: ranking is also weaker than PCA and Standard feature-energy.
But the small healthy calibration tail materially amplifies the weakness.

## 8. Final cross-dataset conclusions

### Supported

1. **PCA reconstruction does not intrinsically cause sparse explanations.**

2. **Explanation concentration is pipeline- and data-dependent.**

   A useful empirical model is:

   ```text
   concentration =
       f(
           data geometry,
           feature construction,
           scaling,
           representation,
           sensor availability,
           fault/state,
           timestamp
       )
   ```

3. **Scaling materially changes both explanation geometry and detector
   operating behavior.**

4. **Explanation dimensionality is time-dependent.**

5. **High explanation dimensionality alone is not a reliable precursor.**

6. **There is no demonstrated universal diffuse-to-concentrated alert-state
   sequence.**

7. **Ranking quality is insufficient for deployment decisions.**

   A deployable anomaly representation should be evaluated on:

   ```text
   ranking quality
   +
   explanation behavior
   +
   calibration stability
   ```

8. **PCA is currently the most defensible anomaly representation in the
   AeroXAI research evidence**, because it combines:

   - strong MetroPT incident detection,
   - explainable reconstruction-error decomposition,
   - cross-asset IITK ranking performance,
   - substantially stronger IITK calibration stability than direct
     feature-energy.

   This does **not** promote a new detector into the frozen V3 release.

### Not supported

Do not claim:

- PCA predicts every compressor fault in advance.
- concentrated detector evidence means a physically simple fault.
- distributed detector evidence means a fault precursor.
- AeroXAI has learned a universal fault-progression state machine.
- IITK validates MetroPT temporal precursor behavior.
- IITK acoustic features reveal physical root cause.
- perfect sampled IITK AUC means population AUC is exactly 1.
- the REAL datasets calibrate the current physics twin.
- the REAL studies validate the simulated energy-saving percentage.

## 9. Research principle for AeroXAI

The strongest research principle produced by this work is:

> **AeroXAI should not judge an anomaly representation by ranking performance
> alone. A deployable representation must also produce interpretable evidence
> and support a statistically stable calibration-to-action boundary.**

This aligns with the proof-carrying architecture:

```text
telemetry
→ anomaly evidence
→ explanation
→ hypothesis
→ physical verification
→ robust action
→ proof bundle
```

The anomaly stage itself therefore needs its own proof obligations:

```text
Does it rank anomalies?
Can operators inspect why?
Is the alert boundary stable enough to operationalize?
```

That is a stronger foundation than treating the anomaly detector as a single
score-producing black box.
